Good to know

The IRS writes first

The IRS usually contacts you by mail first. A call, text or email demanding payment is likely a scam. Call us before you respond.

Had a life change?

Marriage, a new baby, a new job or retirement can change your taxes. Mention it when you book.

Keep your copies

Hold onto returns and records for at least three years in case the IRS has questions later.

Owe? File anyway

Filing on time, even if you can't pay in full, avoids the larger late-filing penalty. We can help set up a payment plan.

Common questions

Do I need an appointment?

In-person visits are by appointment only, so every client gets Connie's full attention. Drop-offs are always welcome. Just leave your documents and we'll call you with any questions.

Will the price change?

Our listed prices are starting prices, and rates may be adjusted from time to time as costs go up. You'll always know your price before any work begins.

How long does it take to get my refund?

Most e-filed returns with direct deposit are processed by the IRS within a few weeks. We'll tell you if anything might slow yours down.

Can you file my state return too?

Yes. Georgia returns are included, and we handle part-year moves and folks who live in Tennessee but work in Georgia.
